Access is intended for individual use. Token sharing, reselling, attempts to bypass usage controls and misuse of the service are not permitted. Please review the applicable terms before activation.

Refund requests can be raised through the configured support contact and may be reviewed against the applicable policy and account usage. Eligibility and outcomes depend on review; a refund is not guaranteed.
